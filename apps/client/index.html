The world

Seven territories joined into one night landscape, from riverside meadow to glowing fen and high crags. Every creature you see is simulated: where it goes, what it eats, and who it trusts.

The species

Every species is built procedurally and rendered under the same moonlight as the world. These are real in-game renders.

    How it works

    1. The world runs without you

      One shared world ticks on a server. Each creature has needs (hunger, thirst, rest, company) and picks its own goals: forage, drink, explore, socialize, court.

    2. Creatures remember

      They keep memories of places and each other, build relationships, and pass traits to their young. Families grow into lineages you can follow over generations.

    3. You nudge, you don't control

      Name a creature or a lineage, favor a bloodline, or enrich and calm a territory. Influence is limited each day, so choices matter.

    4. Everyone sees the same world

      Every player watches the same creatures, positions and clock. Pick a creature to inspect its family, memories and story.
